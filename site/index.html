How it works

Four steps, no drawing.

Say it

12 V to 3.3 V buck, 2 A. Put C1 left of U1. Hints are welcome, wires are not needed.

The agent designs

Your model of choice works through a typed operation API. A deterministic kernel turns intent into geometry.

You review

Every change is a checked, reviewable changeset. ERC and DRC run before you see it. Accept, reject, or ask again.

Get the board

Auto-placed, auto-routed, ground pour, 2D and 3D, exported as Gerber, drill, BOM and pick-and-place.

you

Add a USB-C port for power and pull CC1/CC2 down with 5k1.

agent

Added J1 and the two CC pull-downs. Checked: no new ERC warnings.

Why 4k7

Built for agent workflows, not for clicking.

Correct by construction

Every AI change is a transaction: validated, ERC/DRC-checked, shown as a diff. Nothing lands unchecked.

Bring your own AI, over MCP

Anthropic, OpenAI-compatible, Gemini or local models. 4k7 is an MCP server, so any agent can drive it.

Parts that are real

Drop a datasheet PDF and get symbol and footprint. Source from JLCPCB on demand, LCSC numbers in the BOM.

Intuitive, free of clutter

No toolbars to learn. Chat, canvas and a review of each change; the design owns the screen. Runs locally, keys stay with you. MIT licensed.
