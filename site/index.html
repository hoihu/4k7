4Schematic

It asks first, then reads the datasheets.

A part it doesn't know comes straight from the manufacturer's datasheet: the agent reads the PDF, and you get the symbol, the land pattern and the pin functions. It notes where the datasheet keeps its typical application, design procedure and layout guidelines, designs from those pages, and tells you which page each choice came from.

  • Symbol and land pattern from the datasheet PDF
  • Application notes and design procedure, cited by page
  • Sheets that draw themselves, checked on every change

7Board

A board laid out the way the datasheet says.

The agent places the parts that matter, the placer arranges the rest, and the router keeps going until every connection is made. Around the regulator it follows the datasheet's layout example: a tight input loop, a short switch node, vias under the thermal pad.

  • Placement and routing, down to the last connection
  • Inner planes, pours, lines at a set impedance
  • Gerber, drill, BOM, pick-and-place and STEP

×100Enclosure

Then the box around it.

You agree a brief with the agent — printer, material, pieces, the look. It writes the enclosure as an OpenSCAD script around your board, renders it with OpenSCAD on your computer and fixes what the checks find: a part in the way, a plug that can't reach its opening, no room under the lid.

  • An OpenSCAD script you can read and edit
  • Checked against the board and every plug
  • A 3MF print plate, or one STL per piece
  • Labels and logos, engraved or inlaid

±5 %Order

Priced at three board houses before you order.

Pick JLCPCB, PCBWay or Eurocircuits. 4k7 checks live stock for the number of boards you want, prices the assembly with every fee, and offers parts in stock, or cheaper, a click away. Ask the agent and it goes through the design for savings.

  • Live stock and prices
  • Every assembly fee, itemised
  • Replacements and cheaper values in one click

The same 10 boards, checked live on 26 October 2026. Each total says what it covers: JLCPCB's includes the parts, PCBWay and Eurocircuits price them once you upload the BOM, and none includes the bare boards.
